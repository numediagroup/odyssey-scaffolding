About Odyssey Scaffolding

Professional scaffolding across Greater London & Surrey

At Odyssey Scaffolding we provide safe, reliable and professionally installed scaffolding solutions for domestic and commercial projects throughout Greater London, Surrey and the surrounding areas. With over 15 years of experience, we've built our business around high standards of workmanship, dependable service and a strong commitment to health and safety.

Whether you need scaffolding for a small residential property, a large commercial development, renovation work or ongoing building maintenance, our experienced team provides a scaffolding system designed around the specific requirements of your project. We understand that every site is different — so we assess access, working requirements and site conditions before recommending the most suitable solution.

We work with homeowners, builders, roofing contractors, property developers, construction companies, landlords, managing agents and commercial businesses — on projects of every size.
