Domestic scaffolding

Reliable residential scaffolding across Greater London & Surrey

When work is being carried out on your home, having safe and dependable access can make all the difference to how smoothly the project runs. At Odyssey Scaffolding, we provide professional domestic scaffolding services for homeowners, builders, roofers and other trades throughout Greater London, Surrey and the surrounding areas.

With 15 years of experience in the scaffolding industry, our team can provide practical scaffolding solutions for residential projects of all sizes. From a simple scaffold tower for minor repairs to complete scaffolding around a property for a major renovation, extension or new roof, every installation is carefully planned around the individual property and work being undertaken.

Homeowners, builders, roofers, decorators, window companies, property developers and other trades — residential projects of every size.

What we cover

Scaffolding for all types of residential work

No two properties or projects are exactly the same. Access restrictions, neighbouring properties, extensions, conservatories and the layout of the building can all affect the scaffolding required. Our experienced team will assess your requirements and provide a suitable scaffolding solution designed to create a safe and practical working environment.

Our domestic scaffolding services include:

  • Roofing scaffolding

    Safe access for roof repairs, replacements, tiling, slating, leadwork and general roofing projects.

  • Chimney scaffolding

    Access for chimney repairs, repointing, removal, lead flashing and maintenance.

  • Extension scaffolding

    Scaffolding for single and double-storey home extensions and building projects.

  • Loft conversion scaffolding

    Access solutions for dormers, roof alterations and complete loft conversion projects.

  • Painting & decorating scaffolding

    Safe working platforms for exterior painting, decorating and property maintenance.

  • Rendering & repointing scaffolding

    Suitable access for rendering, plastering, brickwork repairs and repointing.

  • Guttering, fascias & soffits

    Scaffolding for the repair or replacement of roofline products.

  • Window installation scaffolding

    Safe external access for window repairs and replacements.

  • Home renovation scaffolding

    Complete scaffolding solutions for major property refurbishments and renovations.

  • Temporary roof scaffolding

    Helping protect residential properties from the weather during roofing and construction work.

  • New build scaffolding

    Scaffolding solutions for individual new homes and residential development projects.
