Odyssey Scaffolding provides dependable commercial scaffolding solutions throughout Greater London, Surrey and the surrounding areas, supporting businesses, contractors and developers with safe and practical access systems.

What we cover

Scaffolding for commercial & construction projects

Commercial properties can present a wide range of access challenges, from busy high streets and restricted sites to large buildings and ongoing construction projects. We take the time to understand the requirements of each project before providing a suitable scaffolding solution.

Our commercial scaffolding services include:

  • Construction scaffolding

    Scaffolding systems for building contractors and construction companies undertaking projects of various sizes.

  • Commercial property scaffolding

    Access solutions for offices, shops, warehouses and other commercial premises.

  • New build scaffolding

    Scaffolding for new-build residential and commercial developments.

  • Roofing scaffolding

    Safe access for commercial roof repairs, replacement, maintenance and installation.

  • Temporary roof systems

    Helping to protect buildings and active work areas during construction, refurbishment and roofing projects.

  • Refurbishment scaffolding

    Access for commercial renovations, alterations and complete building refurbishments.

  • Painting & decorating scaffolding

    Working platforms for external decoration and property improvement projects.

  • Rendering & cladding scaffolding

    Access solutions for rendering, cladding installation, repairs and maintenance.

  • Brickwork & repointing scaffolding

    Safe access for masonry repairs, repointing and restoration projects.

  • Property maintenance scaffolding

    Short and longer-term scaffolding solutions for planned building maintenance.

  • Development scaffolding

    Scaffolding for property developers undertaking conversions, extensions and larger development projects.

Who we work with

Working with contractors, developers & businesses

Odyssey Scaffolding works with a broad range of commercial clients, including main contractors, builders, roofing companies, property developers, landlords, managing agents, facilities management companies and commercial property owners.

We recognise that scaffolding is often one part of a much larger project. Delays in erection or changes to access can affect other trades and potentially impact the overall schedule. Our approach is therefore based around good communication, careful planning and providing a reliable service from initial installation through to dismantling.

Where a project develops or access requirements change, we can work alongside contractors to adapt the scaffolding where appropriate.
